# Wagyu's Android checklist

Use this on a phone with the Wagyu field guide (**Home → Try it: the Wagyu field guide**), the same master test
file as the VS Code extension's [checklist](https://github.com/Orexii/ai-markdown-reader/blob/main/examples/wagyu-checklist.md).
Its source also has a hidden note next to each feature (an HTML comment starting with "Check:") that says what
you should hear. On a foldable, do sections 1 to 3 on both screens. On a wide screen (unfolded, or a tablet), the
page and the player's buttons stay together in the middle.

## 1. Start reading

- [ ] The first time, the player offers the natural voice: **Download (92 MB)**, **Best (326 MB)** or **Use the
  phone's voice instead**. Download it: the percentage counts up, then the voice starts.
- [ ] Press Play. The first sentence is highlighted on the page and the word being spoken more strongly. The
  highlight moves word by word, in time with the voice.
- [ ] The page scrolls to keep the reading in view. Scroll far away: it stops following, and **Back to reading**
  appears at the bottom. Tap it: it follows again.
- [ ] The player shows the section heading, the progress bar with marks at the headings, the time read and the time
  left (with ~ while it is estimated).

## 2. Buttons and speed

- [ ] ▶| goes to the next paragraph; in the Quick facts table it goes to the table's introduction first, then one row
  at a time. |◀ goes to the start of this paragraph or row, then (tapped again) the one before. At the last
  paragraph, ▶| says "This is the last paragraph".
- [ ] ↺ 10 and 30 ↻ skip by seconds. Dragging the progress bar moves the reading there.
- [ ] The speed menu offers 0.5×, 1×, 1.5× and 2×. **More speeds…** lists every quarter step from 0.25× to 5×, and
  **Fewer speeds** goes back. The pitch stays natural at every speed.
- [ ] At 3× the reading never stops to wait; at 5×, the long sentence in Edge cases plays without stopping where it
  is split (a short wait at the very start is fine).
- [ ] The phone's volume buttons change the volume.

## 3. With the screen off and with headphones

- [ ] Lock the phone while reading: it keeps reading. The lock screen and the notification show the document and the
  section, with play/pause, back 10 and forward 30. Unlock and the page has followed along.
- [ ] Bluetooth headphones: play/pause works; next and previous track skip by paragraph.
- [ ] Unplug or disconnect the headphones: reading pauses.
- [ ] A phone call or another app playing audio pauses the reading; it resumes after a short interruption.
- [ ] Close the app (swipe it away) while paused: the notification goes. While reading, reading continues.

## 4. What you should hear

- [ ] The front matter and the HTML comments are never read.
- [ ] Meet Wagyu: "8 to 11 kilograms", "100 percent", "12 dollars and 50 cents", "version 2 point 4 point 1",
  "count Zoomies", "Shiba Zoom Tracker", "zoomies per day"; I O T, JSON and H T T P S are spelled out where needed.
- [ ] The mailing address is read one line at a time, with a short pause after each line.
- [ ] The table, quotes, alerts, numbered and nested lists, task list and tricks are read as the hidden notes say.
- [ ] Code blocks are announced with their language and length; set **Code blocks** to **Read aloud** in the settings
  (⚙) and they are read line by line; set it back to **Announce**. The reading keeps its place each time.
- [ ] Images are read as "Image:" and their description. Links are read by their text, never their address.
- [ ] "2 times 3 equals 6", and the emoji are silent.

## 5. Moving around in the page

- [ ] Double-tap "Yellow" in the Quick facts table: reading jumps to that row.
- [ ] Long-press a word, then choose **Read aloud from here**: reading starts there.
- [ ] Select the Meet Wagyu section and choose **Read selection aloud**: only the selection is read, then reading stops.
- [ ] The Contents links scroll to each section; the Wikipedia link opens the browser; **the short demo** opens
  `demo.md` in the app.
- [ ] Table rows and code blocks are highlighted as a whole while they are read; wide tables scroll sideways.
- [ ] With a keyboard: → and Shift+→ move by paragraph (in the table, by row) and sentence, Shift+↓ by section, K plays
  and pauses.

## 6. Settings

- [ ] **Tables: Column by column**: the table is read one column at a time. Set it back.
- [ ] Turn off **Highlight each word**: only the sentence is highlighted. Turn it back on.
- [ ] Turn off **Keep the reading in view**: the page no longer scrolls along. Turn it back on.
- [ ] Choose another voice, for example **Emma · UK female**: it downloads (0.5 MB) and reading continues in her voice
  with British pronunciation.
- [ ] **Phone's voice** reads with the phone's own voice (words highlighted approximately); **Natural** switches back.
- [ ] Add a pronunciation, for example **Wagyu → wah gyoo**: the next time Wagyu is read, it is said that way.
- [ ] **Theme: Dark** and **Light** change the app and the page. The status bar's icons stay readable (light on
  Dark, dark on Light), even when the phone's own theme is the other one.

## 7. Files and other apps

- [ ] **Open a Markdown file** and pick a .md file: it opens and reads. It appears under Recent.
- [ ] **Open a folder** with Markdown and images: the files are listed; opening one shows its images, and links to
  other files in the folder open them.
- [ ] In a file manager or Gmail, open a .md file **with AI Markdown Reader**, and **Share** one to it.
- [ ] Share some text from a web page or a note to AI Markdown Reader: it is read as a document.

## 8. Finish

- [ ] Go back to Home and open the Wagyu guide again: reading resumes where you stopped.
- [ ] **Settings → Remove the downloaded natural voice**: the player offers the download again.

Good dog. 🐕
